Melissa's Portfolio Website

Purpose, Audience and Goal

Purpose

My personal website project will act as a professional portfolio that showcases my skills, talent and expertise as a burgeoning Communications and Web Development freelancer.

Audience

The primary visitors of my website will be hiring managers, recruiters or small business owners looking to hire someone with the skills I possess. This website will allow these prospective employers to easily view past professional work I have produced. Additionally, professional peers such as coworkers or collaborators of mine may peruse the website to gain insight into what capabilities I have.

Goal

My primary goal is to produce a website that helps me land fractional and freelance work. The website should make me a more competitive candidate in the current marketplace by showcasing prior projects I have produced and providing a contact form through which visitors can get in touch with me.
