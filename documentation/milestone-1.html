Melissa's Portfolio Website

Purpose, Audience and Goal

Purpose

My personal website project will act as a professional portfolio that showcases my skills, talent and expertise as a burgeoning Communications and Web Development freelancer.

Audience

The primary visitors of my website will be hiring managers, recruiters or small business owners looking to hire someone with the skills I possess. This website will allow these prospective employers to easily view past professional work I have produced. Additionally, professional peers such as coworkers or collaborators of mine may peruse the website to gain insight into what capabilities I have.

Goal

My primary goal is to produce a website that helps me land fractional and freelance work. The website should make me a more competitive candidate in the current marketplace by showcasing prior projects I have produced and providing a contact form through which visitors can get in touch with me.

Persona

Name:

Holly

Age:

40

Profession:

Founder, CEO and sole employee of a new nonprofit focused on animal welfare. The nonprofit is a new venture and after recently receiving a state grant for the nonprofit, Holly decides to hire additional help.

Technology:

MacBook Air 2025 M4 used primarily at home or cafes; iPhone 16e with an unlimited talk, text and data plan.

Attitudes and Behaviors:

Holly is very busy working full-time to get this nonprofit off the ground. She is quickly and actively searching for someone to bring on in a freelance position to help with the organization. To keep the hiring search as efficient as possible she only follows up with candidates who have contact information clearly listed on their resumes, portfolios, or websites. She will discard a resume if no phone number, email, or other mode of contact is visibly listed.

Frustrations and Needs:

Holly would like to bring someone on to help with building this organization. Specifically she's hoping to find a freelancer who can help in a variety of areas, ranging from marketing and communications to graphic design to web development. She is on a tight budget due to the typical financial constraints of a small nonprofit and would like to find one talented generalist who can perform work in all these areas.

Goals

Holly wants to determine, after a short review of the website (~5 min), whether a candidate meets the qualifications to work in a freelance role for her nonprofit.

User Story:

As an entrepreneurial user, I need to quickly determine whether this person would be an appropriate hire for my new nonprofit -- and I need an easy way to contact them.
